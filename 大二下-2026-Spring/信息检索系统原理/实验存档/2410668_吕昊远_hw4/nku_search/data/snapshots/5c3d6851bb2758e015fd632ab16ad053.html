Nankai and NEOMA restarted the China-Eu Management Innovation Forum

2023-11-10

On November 9, 2023, the China-Eu Management Innovation Forum hosted by the Nankai Business School and co-organized by the NEOMA Business School of France was once again held in Nankai University. The forum aims to bring together management scholars and government and enterprise practitioners at home and abroad to jointly study management and innovation in the context of internationalization and the new era of artificial intelligence, and promote academic exchanges and cooperation. Wang Jian, Secretary of the Party Committee, Bai Changhong, Dean, Lin Runhui, Tian Li, Li Kai, Vice deans, Fouad BEN, Distinguished Professor, Nool Business School, France ABDELAZIZ, Luo Xin, Distinguished Professor of the University of New Mexico, Zhang Ping, Chairman of Tianjin Risen Pharmaceutical Co., LTD., Zhang Shaochen, Deputy General manager of the Tianjin Government and Enterprise Business Department of Huawei, Yang Huajun, General manager of Tik Tok Group Tianjin, Zhang Nan, general manager of Sensetime Intelligent Technology Co., LTD.,attended the forum.https://bs.nankai.edu.cn/2023/1110/c9007a528034/page.htm
