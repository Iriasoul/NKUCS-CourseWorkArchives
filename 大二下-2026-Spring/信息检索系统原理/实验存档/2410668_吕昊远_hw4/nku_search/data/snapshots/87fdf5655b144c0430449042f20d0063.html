Cui Xuewei

发布者：李雁伟发布时间：2021-11-08浏览次数：64

一、Research Area

Basic Theory of Civil Law, Land Law.

 

二、Biography

Cui Xuewei, the lecturer of NanKai University School of Law from Baoding, Hebei province. Dr.cui studied in Central South University from September 2011 to June 2015 and obtained a Bachelor of Laws degree; From September 2015 to June 2021, she continued her study in the University for further study and obtained the doctorate degree of Law through the combination of master and doctoral studies; From October 2018 to October 2019, she went to the Law School of The University of Groningen in the Netherlands for an exchange visit.

When it comes to scientific research projects, Cui once presided over the Innovation Project of Central South University Research on the Problems and Legislative Improvement of the Business Model of Three Rights of Agricultural Land, Project No. : 2017ZZTS251; And Innovation Project of Central South University: Research on The Legal System of Rural Collective Land Expropriation compensation, Project No. : 2018ZZTS087;
