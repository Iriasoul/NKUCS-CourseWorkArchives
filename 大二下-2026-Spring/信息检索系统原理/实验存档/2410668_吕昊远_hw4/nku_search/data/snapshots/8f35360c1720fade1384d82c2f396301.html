DBIS2023级研究生报名

Ying Zhang (张莹)
She received the Ph.D. degree from Nankai University, Tianjin, China, in 2013. From 2011 to 2013, She studied at Purdue University, West Lafayette, IN, USA. She is currently a Professor with the College of Computer Science, Nankai University. Her main research interests include sentiment analysis, multimodal data analysis, and information retrieval.

Database and Information System Lab (DBIS)
Colledge of Computer Science,Nankai University

News | Research Interest | Service and Activities |Publications |Awards

个人介绍

    张莹，教授，博士生导师，国家级青年科学人才，天津市杰出青年科学基金获得者，南开大学百名青年学科带头人（A计划），计算机学院院长助理，主要研究方向为网络空间安全治理、多模态数据挖掘、情感分析、意图识别等。近五年在IEEE TIP、IEEE TMM、IEEE TNNLS、NeurIPS、ACL等高水平会议和期刊发表论文40余篇，主持国家级、省部级重点课题10余项。
