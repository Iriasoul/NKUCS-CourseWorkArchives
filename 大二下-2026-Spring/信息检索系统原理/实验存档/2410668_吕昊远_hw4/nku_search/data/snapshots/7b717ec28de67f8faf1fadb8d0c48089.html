The Basketball Team of the School Wins the Championship of the 32nd “The President’s Cup”

发布者：戴建芳发布时间：2021-05-24浏览次数：13

May 22nd was a day out of common for the basketball team of the School of Physics. After winning the championship of Bailitai Campus for the first time, the team forged ahead for the championship of “The President’s Cup” Basketball Game of Nankai University. It was a trophy earned with difficulties for the School of Physics as the opponent, the team from the School of Electronic Information and Optical Engineering, was an established and competitive one who demonstrated excellent skills and strategies during the game. We sincerely appreciated their excellence and extended our gratitude to them as the players from both sides offered us a remarkable final that was impressive enough to be counted as one of the most extraordinary ones in all “The President Cup” games.
