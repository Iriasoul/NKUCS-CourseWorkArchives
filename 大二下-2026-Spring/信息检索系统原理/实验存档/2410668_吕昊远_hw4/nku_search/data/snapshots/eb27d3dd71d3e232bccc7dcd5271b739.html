Department of Finance

Committed to research and teaching core finance courses, including finance, international finance, monetary banking, financial markets, and banking (management)

Department of Quantitative Finance

Committed to research and teaching related to financial big data analysis and computing, such as financial engineering, financial econometrics, financial data analysis, mathematical finance, financial mathematics, and quantitative trading

Department of Applied Finance

Committed to research and teaching in investment analysis, corporate finance, asset pricing, financial leasing, wealth management, asset management and other emerging financial domains

Department of Financial Economics

Committed to research and teaching in fundamental areas in economics and finance, such as modern economics, and financial economics

Department of Risk Management and Insurance

Committed to research and nurturing talents in insurance

Department of Actuarial Science

Committed to research and teaching in actuarial science

Institute of Financial Research  

Institute of Finance Research focuses on academic research of finance theory, practice, and policy. It is also a think tank and helps the School of Finance to cultivate talents in finance

Institute of Digital Finance

Conduct academic, policy and industry research in multi-disciplinary fields such as digital finance and financial technology (FinTech), keep track of the digital transformation of social assets, serve the government and industry, and cultivate research experts

Institute of Risk Management and Insurance   

Conduct research on theories and applications of risk management, insurance and actuarial science, provide consultation for national policy-making and corporate operations, and cultivate research experts

Institute of Pension and Health Security

Conduct research on pension finance, health industry, medical security and long-term healthcare security, and cultivate research experts
