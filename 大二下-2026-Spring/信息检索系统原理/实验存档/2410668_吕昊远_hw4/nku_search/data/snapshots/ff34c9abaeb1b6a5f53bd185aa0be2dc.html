Closing the Gender Gap in Multilateral Negotiations Through Institution Design

Speaker: Assistant Professor Diogo Geraldes

Time: 2:30 p.m., October 18, 2024 (Friday)

Place: Conference Room on the 11th Floor, Office Building, School of Economics


More Information:

Diogo Geraldes, PhD from Maastricht University in the Netherlands, is currently an assistant professor at School of Economics, University College Dublin, Ireland. His research areas include Behavioral Economics, Experimental Economics and Game Theory.
