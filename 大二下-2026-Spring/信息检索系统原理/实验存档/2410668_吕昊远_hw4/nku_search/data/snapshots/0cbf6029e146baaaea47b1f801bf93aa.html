Life in TEDA
　 Overview of campus 　

TEDA is a great place to live and our campus is located in the heart of TEDA. Whether you are new to TEDA, live here already or are looking for somewhere to mix up your day-to-day routine, our campus is fun to explore.

  


● Eating and Living in TEDA College

We provide students and staff with fantastic places to eat and live.

Refectory

The refectory is the main catering outlet on campus, offering a variety of food with different flavors all over the country.


Accommodation

We offer fantastic accommodation available to the students from different schools and institutes. 

● Sport facilities

At TEDA you'll be able to get involved in a range of sports from basketball and tennis, to badminton, ping-pong, and table tennis. You could access our state of all the sport facilities by making a reservation.

  


●  Green Spaces

There are a number of public green spaces on campus for everyone to enjoy.

         

● Library

The TEDA College Library is located in the heart of our campus.It contains all kinds of collections for our students and staff.
